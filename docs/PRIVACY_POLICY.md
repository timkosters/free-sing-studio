# Singwell Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

Singwell is a singing practice app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your audio never leaves your device, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

## Microphone and recordings

Singwell uses the microphone to detect the pitch you are singing and, when you press Record, to save a recording. All processing happens on your device. Recordings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and are included in your normal iPhone backup. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. You can delete any recording from the Library, or delete the app to remove them all.

## Practice history

Your practice time, observed vocal range, quest results and daily streak are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time from the Progress tab.

## Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides the app with a stable user identifier and, only on first sign-in, your name and email address if you agree to share them. These are stored on your device so the app can greet you and, in a future update, sync your data between your own devices. They are not sent to us, used for marketing, or shared with third parties. You can use the whole app as a guest without signing in.

## Purchases

Singwell Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. No notification data leaves the device.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no servers, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party trackers. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized App Store statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

## Children

Singwell is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated date.
