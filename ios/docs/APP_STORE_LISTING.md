# App Store listing copy (draft)

**Name (30 chars max):** Singwell

**Subtitle (30):** See your voice. Sing in tune.

**Promotional text (170):** Live pitch on a piano roll, a ten-minute daily routine, and recordings you can actually learn from. Audio never leaves your phone.

**Description:**

Singwell shows you your voice while you sing. Every note lands on a chromatic piano roll in real time, with the frequency and how many cents you are above or below. Tap any key to hear the reference. Record a take and listen back with the pitch trail attached.

TEN MINUTES A DAY
A guided routine built from what vocal coaches actually ask for: wake the body, breathe low, close the folds cleanly, glide across the break between chest and head voice, then one section of a song. Each step has a clock, a cue and the reason it is there. Keep a streak.

WARM-UPS
Call-and-response arpeggios and five-note scales. The piano demonstrates, a count-in cues you, you sing it back, then up a semitone. Every note you sing is labelled Hit, Low, High or Keep steady.

PITCH QUEST
One target note at a time. Match it within 50 cents and hold for a second. Targets never leap more than five semitones and never leave the range you set.

YOUR RANGE
Only notes held for half a second count. See today's range against your all-time range on a map from C2 to C6, and share a range card.

YOUR TAKES
Recordings live in your Library with the pitch trail, so in a month you can hear the difference. Rename, favorite, share or export as audio.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts required, no uploads, no analytics. Sign in with Apple is optional. Everything stays on your iPhone.

SINGWELL PRO
Free includes the live pitch roll, reference piano, three saved takes, the Quick routine and short quests. Pro removes every limit: unlimited takes, the Full and Bridge routines, every drill and long quests. Monthly or yearly with a free week, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Use headphones during warm-ups and quests so the microphone hears only you.

**App Review notes:**
Guest mode works without an account; no test credentials needed. To see the pitch display without singing, open the Sing tab, tap the sliders icon top right, and choose "Try demo voice". All audio processing is on-device; the app makes no network requests except StoreKit and Sign in with Apple. Subscriptions use StoreKit 2 with a restore button on the paywall and in Account.

**Privacy nutrition label:** Data Not Collected for everything except Sign in with Apple identifiers (Name, Email, User ID), declared as "used for App Functionality, not linked, not used for tracking, stored on device".
